Every builder in Seaton lands in roughly the same place on total deposit — around 10% of purchase price. Where they differ, enormously, is how long you have to pay it and how big the first cheque is. Deposits are also set per release, so your neighbour's schedule from last spring may not be yours. We'll walk you through the actual schedule before you sign anything.
